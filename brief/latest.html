  • If the Agioi Fanendes returns to Russia with the cargo undelivered, the specific thesis is the Trump–Putin scheme has failed to deliver US volumes, which specifically tightens the European physical balance for diesel (Russian refineries have to hold the barrels, limiting export capacity rebalancing).
